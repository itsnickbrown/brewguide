Questions

What grind should I use for a French press?

Coarse, like raw sugar or sea salt. Too fine and the cup turns muddy and bitter, and the plunger gets hard to push. The tool gives a starting setting for your grinder.

How long should a French press steep?

About 4 minutes, then break the crust and wait about 5 more minutes before you plunge. The wait lets the fine particles sink, so the cup is cleaner.

Should I press the plunger all the way down?

No. Press just to the surface of the coffee. Pushing to the bottom stirs up the sludge you just let settle.

What ratio for French press?

Somewhere around 1:15 to 1:16, so 15 g of coffee for about 240 g of water. Tighten it for more body.
