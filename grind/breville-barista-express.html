Breville Barista Express (built-in grinder)

How to read the dial

Grind size dial on the side, 1 (fine) to 16. Breville suggests starting at 5. If you run out of range, the inner burr can be moved too (see Breville's manual).

Adjust for your coffee

  • Fast, sour shot? One step finer. Slow, bitter shot? One step coarser.
  • On time but sour? Pull a longer shot. On time but bitter? Pull a shorter shot.
  • Sour or thin? One step finer. Bitter or dry? One step coarser. Change one thing per brew.

Where these numbers come from

Each setting starts from Breville's own guide for this grinder. Burrs and calibration vary between units, so treat these as a place to start.
