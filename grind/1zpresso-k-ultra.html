How to read the dial

Read it as number.click on the outer dial, so 7.5 means the dot on 7 plus 5 clicks. Each click is 20 microns. Common starting points are about 2.5 for espresso and 7 to 9.5 for pour over.

Adjust for your coffee

  • Floral or bright coffees (washed Ethiopia, Kenya) for pour over: one step finer.
  • Fruity naturals, heavy-bodied or dark coffees for pour over: one step coarser.
  • On the Aiden, keep the setting above. Fellow's own Drops profiles barely change the grind by flavor.
  • Sour or thin? One step finer. Bitter or dry? One step coarser. Change one thing per brew.

Where these numbers come from

Each setting starts from 1Zpresso's own guide for this grinder. The Aiden settings are checked against about 200 of Fellow's own Drops brew profiles. Burrs and calibration vary between units, so treat these as a place to start.
