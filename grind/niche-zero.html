How to read the dial

Stepless dial. Niche suggests about 15 for espresso and 35 to 50 for V60. Units vary, so treat these as a start.

Adjust for your coffee

  • Floral or bright coffees (washed Ethiopia, Kenya) for pour over: one step finer.
  • Fruity naturals, heavy-bodied or dark coffees for pour over: one step coarser.
  • On the Fellow Aiden, keep the setting above. Fellow's own Drops profiles barely change the grind by flavor.
  • Sour or thin? One step finer. Bitter or dry? One step coarser. Change one thing per brew.

Where these numbers come from

Each setting starts from Niche's own guide for this grinder. French press and AeroPress settings use common starting points where the maker doesn't list one. The Aiden settings are checked against about 200 of Fellow's own Drops brew profiles. Burrs and calibration vary between units, so treat these as a place to start.
