How to read the dial

The EG-1 has no fixed zero, so count from burr lock: close the burrs until they just touch, note that number, then add the setting shown. Each tick is 5 microns. Settings assume the CORE burrs. With ULTRA burrs, start a couple of numbers coarser for filter.

Adjust for your coffee

  • Floral or bright coffees (washed Ethiopia, Kenya) for pour over: one step finer.
  • Fruity naturals, heavy-bodied or dark coffees for pour over: one step coarser.
  • On the Aiden, keep the setting above. Fellow's own Drops profiles barely change the grind by flavor.
  • Sour or thin? One step finer. Bitter or dry? One step coarser. Change one thing per brew.

Where these numbers come from

Each setting starts from Weber's own guide for this grinder. The Aiden settings are checked against about 200 of Fellow's own Drops brew profiles. Burrs and calibration vary between units, so treat these as a place to start.
