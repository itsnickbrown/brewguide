How to read the dial

Read it as macro number plus micro letter, so 5M means macro 5, micro M. Baratza's steel burr chart: espresso 1Q, AeroPress 3M, V60 5M, drip 6M, Chemex 7M, French press 9M. About 12 micro letters equal one macro step. Steel burrs may need a finer calibration for espresso. Baratza's Forte manual lists numbers about one macro higher (V60 at 6M), so on a Forte BG, start one macro coarser than shown.

Adjust for your coffee

  • Floral or bright coffees (washed Ethiopia, Kenya) for pour over: one step finer.
  • Fruity naturals, heavy-bodied or dark coffees for pour over: one step coarser.
  • On the Fellow Aiden, keep the setting above. Fellow's own Drops profiles barely change the grind by flavor.
  • Sour or thin? One step finer. Bitter or dry? One step coarser. Change one thing per brew.

Where these numbers come from

Each setting starts from Baratza's own guide for this grinder. French press and AeroPress settings use common starting points where the maker doesn't list one. The Aiden settings are checked against about 200 of Fellow's own Drops brew profiles. Burrs and calibration vary between units, so treat these as a place to start.
